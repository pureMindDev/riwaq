Riwaq: letter recordings, sources and licences

Each file is the spoken NAME of one Arabic letter (e.g. "alif", "baa"), recorded by a native Arabic speaker through Lingua Libre and published on Wikimedia Commons.
They are not articulation (makhraj) demonstrations and they are NOT Tajweed-verified.
Changes made by us/our source: each clip was trimmed to the name, with short silence at each end, loudness-levelled and encoded as AAC (.m4a). The copies were taken with these edits from the credited public repository https://github.com/techjewel/kiddo (sounds/arabic/names/, sounds/CREDITS.md), which documents the same originals and licences.

EXCEPTION (temporary): Bā’ (ba.m4a) is NOT a Lingua Libre recording. It is a clip cut from "حروف الأبجدية العربية Arabic alphabet.ogg", a recording of the whole alphabet by Ibraheem alex on Wikimedia Commons, under the GNU Free Documentation License 1.2 or later (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/fdl-1.2.html: credit the author, note the changes, keep this clip under the same licence). It replaces an earlier Bā’ clip that was only a very short pop with no vowel. Changes: cut to the letter, trimmed, loudness-levelled, mono AAC. It is used until a dedicated, openly licensed Bā’ recording is found.
